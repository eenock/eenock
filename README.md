## Selected work

> Some projects live in private repositories for thesis or competition work. The descriptions are included for context.

### `01` · PreFed-IDS
**Byzantine-Robust Federated Learning for Intrusion Detection**  
Master's thesis project using trust-aware aggregation and DP-SGD through Opacus on a Flower-based federated learning pipeline. Evaluated on CIC-IDS2017 and UNSW-NB15 for resilience against poisoning attacks.

`Python` `PyTorch` `Flower` `Opacus`

### `02` · JiaYou-AufGehts
**German Language Learning Platform**  
Full-stack platform for Chinese users learning German, with integrated WeChat Pay, Alipay, and card payments through Airwallex.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Sanity` `Airwallex`

### `03` · LearnLink 学桥
**AI-Powered Digital Literacy Platform**  
Digital literacy platform targeting learners in Uganda, built for the African Youth AI Case Innovation Competition.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `AI / LLM Integration`

### `04` · AfroCraft
**African Crafts Marketplace**  
E-commerce platform for African crafts, with Stripe-powered checkout and a CMS-driven product catalog.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Sanity` `Stripe`

### `05` · Financial News Sentiment Analysis & Stock Prediction
Bachelor's thesis project using FinBERT, VADER, and LSTM architectures for sentiment-driven stock prediction on more than **1.8 million financial news headlines**.

`Python` `FinBERT` `LSTM`
